# About Me

Hi, I'm **Ashyfox**, a graduate student at **East China Normal University (ECNU)** in Shanghai. I conduct research with the [Decision Intelligence Lab](https://decisionintelligence.github.io/index).

My research focuses on machine learning for spatiotemporal systems. I am particularly interested in building reliable models for incomplete and complex real-world data.

## Research Interests

- **Spatiotemporal Data Mining** — learning spatial and temporal patterns from real-world data.
- **Diffusion Models** — developing generative methods for data reconstruction and imputation.
- **Intelligent Transportation** — applying machine learning to traffic data and mobility systems.
